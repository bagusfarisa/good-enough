# Prepare library pickup holds

Start with the current reservation list. Check that the item barcode matches the reservation. Put a matched item with an active reservation on the pickup shelf and scan the shelf slot in the pickup system. Send the standard pickup notice after the slot appears in the reservation record.

## Item unavailable after label printing

If the item cannot be located after the pickup label has been printed, pause that reservation. Keep the label at the desk and put the reservation in Search pending. Tell the Collections desk to investigate. The Collections desk decides whether a replacement item is available. When it confirms the replacement and the reservation is cleared to proceed, restart at the item barcode check. Use the confirmed replacement barcode and retain the old label at the desk until the new shelf slot is recorded.

## Reservation expires during preparation

If the reservation expires before the pickup notice is sent, stop preparation. Set Expired review. Ask Member Services whether the reservation can be reinstated. Keep any item behind the desk until Member Services responds. Do not send a pickup notice on an expired reservation.

## Handoff and exceptions

Collections handles item searches and replacements. Member Services handles reinstatement decisions. Ask the Circulation process owner about unresolved exceptions. At the end of the shift, hand off every open search or review with the reservation ID and its recorded owner.
