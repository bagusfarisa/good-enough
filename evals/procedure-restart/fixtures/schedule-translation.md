# Publish regional workshop schedules

Use the translation workbook approved by Localization. Confirm the Workbook version field matches the approval record. Generate a draft schedule with Build schedule. Compare the dates, venue names, and registration links against the workbook in Review draft. Keep the last published schedule live until the replacement passes review. Publish once and record the version and publication time.

## Hold for disputed translation

If Review draft finds disputed wording, set Editorial hold and leave the current schedule live. Refer the disputed phrase and workbook version to the Regional editor. Keep the hold until the Regional editor confirms the disposition in writing. Retain that confirmation with the workbook.

## Hold for a broken registration link

If Review draft finds a broken registration link, leave the draft unpublished and notify the Registration team. Replace the link in the draft when Registration supplies the approved URL. Resume at Review draft and repeat all three comparisons. If the wording is also disputed, keep Editorial hold until the Regional editor confirms in writing.

## Ownership and publication record

The Regional editor owns disputed wording. Registration owns approved registration URLs. The Schedule procedure owner handles questions the publishing procedure leaves unresolved. Record the version and publication time after checking the public schedule displays the approved dates, venue names, and registration links.
