# Prepare library pickup holds

**Prerequisite:** Have the current reservation list open.

1. Check that the item barcode matches the reservation and that the reservation is active.
2. Put the item on the pickup shelf and scan the shelf slot in the pickup system.
3. Confirm that the shelf slot appears in the reservation record. Check that the reservation is still active, then send the standard pickup notice.

## Item missing after label printing

Pause the reservation, keep the printed label at the desk, and set the status to **Search pending**. Ask the Collections desk to investigate. Collections decides whether a replacement is available.

Once Collections confirms a replacement and the reservation is cleared to proceed, restart at step 1 with the confirmed replacement barcode. Keep the old label at the desk until the new shelf slot is recorded.

## Reservation expires before the notice is sent

Stop preparation, set the status to **Expired review**, and ask Member Services whether the reservation can be reinstated. Keep any item behind the desk until Member Services responds. Send a pickup notice only if the reservation is active and the shelf slot is recorded.

## Shift handoff

At the end of the shift, hand off every open search or review with its reservation ID and recorded owner. Refer unresolved exceptions to the Circulation process owner.
