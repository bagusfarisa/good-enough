# Prepare library pickup holds

Start with the current reservation list.

1. Check that the item barcode matches the reservation and that the reservation is active.
2. Put the matched item on the pickup shelf and scan the shelf slot in the pickup system.
3. Confirm that the shelf slot appears in the reservation record and that the reservation is still active. Then send the standard pickup notice.

Preparation is complete when the reservation record shows the shelf slot and you have sent the pickup notice.

## If you cannot find the item after printing its label

1. Pause preparation, keep the label at the desk, and set the reservation status to **Search pending**.
2. Ask the Collections desk to investigate. Collections decides whether a replacement is available.
3. When Collections confirms a replacement and the reservation is cleared to proceed, restart at step 1 with the confirmed replacement barcode. Keep the old label at the desk until the new shelf slot appears in the reservation record.

## If the reservation expires before you send the notice

1. Stop preparation, set the reservation status to **Expired review**, and keep any item behind the desk until Member Services responds.
2. Ask Member Services whether the reservation can be reinstated. Do not send a pickup notice while the reservation is expired.
3. If Member Services reinstates the reservation, ask the Circulation process owner where to restart. If the reservation expires while a search is open, ask the process owner how to handle both statuses.

## End-of-shift handoff

Hand off every open search or review with the reservation ID and the recorded owner. Refer any other unresolved exceptions to the Circulation process owner.
