# Prepare library pickup holds

## Before you start

Use the current reservation list.

## Prepare each hold

1. Check that the item barcode matches the reservation and that the reservation is active. If you printed the pickup label but cannot locate the item, follow the search procedure below.
2. Put the matched item on the pickup shelf and scan the shelf slot in the pickup system.
3. Confirm that the scanned slot appears in the reservation record. If the reservation is still active, send the standard pickup notice.

If the reservation expires at any point before you send the notice, follow the expiry procedure below.

## Item unavailable after label printing

1. Pause preparation, keep the printed label at the desk, and set the reservation status to **Search pending**.
2. Ask the Collections desk to investigate. Collections decides whether a replacement is available.
3. When Collections confirms a replacement and the reservation is cleared to proceed, restart at step 1 using the confirmed replacement barcode. Keep the old label at the desk until the new shelf slot appears in the reservation record.

## Reservation expires before the notice is sent

1. Stop preparation, withhold the pickup notice, and set the reservation status to **Expired review**.
2. Ask Member Services whether the reservation can be reinstated. Keep any item behind the desk until Member Services responds.

## Escalation and shift handoff

Ask the Circulation process owner about unresolved exceptions. At the end of the shift, hand off every open search or review with the reservation ID and the recorded owner.
