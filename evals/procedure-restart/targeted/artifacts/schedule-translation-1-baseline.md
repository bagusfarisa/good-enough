# Publish regional workshop schedules

## Before you start

Use the translation workbook approved by Localization. Confirm that the **Workbook version** field matches the approval record.

## Build and review the draft

1. Run **Build schedule** to generate the draft.
2. In **Review draft**, compare the dates, venue names, and registration links with the approved workbook. Check that the registration links work. Keep the last published schedule live until the replacement passes review.
3. Resolve any issues found during review.

   | Issue | Action | When to continue |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Disputed wording | Set **Editorial hold**. Send the disputed phrase and workbook version to the Regional editor, who owns the wording decision. | Keep the hold until the Regional editor confirms the decision in writing. Retain the confirmation with the workbook. |
   | Broken registration link | Keep the draft unpublished and notify the Registration team, which owns approved registration URLs. Replace the link in the draft with the approved URL supplied by Registration. | Return to **Review draft** and repeat all three comparisons, using the approved replacement URL for the corrected link. If wording is also disputed, keep **Editorial hold** in place until the Regional editor confirms the decision in writing. |

   Refer questions the procedure leaves unresolved to the Schedule procedure owner.

## Publish and verify

1. Once the replacement passes review and any **Editorial hold** is cleared, publish the schedule once.
2. Open the public schedule and verify that it displays the approved dates, venue names, and registration links.
3. After the public check passes, record the workbook version and publication time.
