# Publish regional workshop schedules

Use the translation workbook approved by Localization. Before building the draft, confirm that its **Workbook version** field matches the approval record.

1. Run **Build schedule** to generate a draft.
2. In **Review draft**, compare the dates, venue names, and registration links with the approved workbook. Keep the last published schedule live until the replacement passes review.

   - **Disputed wording.** Set **Editorial hold**. Send the disputed phrase and workbook version to the Regional editor, who owns the wording decision. Keep the hold until the editor confirms the decision in writing. Retain the confirmation with the workbook. Ask the Schedule procedure owner where to resume after the hold is lifted.
   - **Broken registration link.** Leave the draft unpublished and notify the Registration team, which owns approved registration URLs. When Registration supplies the approved URL, replace the link in the draft. Resume at **Review draft** and repeat all three comparisons, using the approved replacement URL for the corrected link.
   - **Both issues in the same draft.** Keep **Editorial hold** until the Regional editor confirms the decision in writing. Ask the Schedule procedure owner whether to repeat **Review draft** as soon as the link is corrected or wait until the hold is lifted. Both issues must be resolved before publication.

3. Once the draft passes review and any hold is lifted, publish it once.
4. Open the public schedule and verify that it displays the approved dates, venue names, and registration links. After the check passes, record the workbook version and publication time.
