# Publish regional workshop schedules

## Prerequisite

Use the translation workbook approved by Localization. Before building the draft, verify that the **Workbook version** field matches the approval record.

## Procedure

1. Run **Build schedule** to generate a draft.
2. In **Review draft**, compare the dates, venue names, and registration links with the approved workbook. Check that the registration links work. Keep the last published schedule live until the replacement passes review.
   - **Disputed wording.** Set **Editorial hold** and send the disputed phrase and workbook version to the Regional editor, who owns the wording decision. Keep the hold until the editor confirms the decision in writing. Retain the confirmation with the workbook.
   - **Broken registration link.** Keep the draft unpublished and notify the Registration team, which owns approved registration URLs. Replace the broken link in the draft with the approved URL the team supplies. Return to **Review draft** and repeat all three comparisons, using Registration’s approved URL for the replacement link. If wording is also disputed, keep **Editorial hold** until the Regional editor confirms the decision in writing.
3. Once the replacement passes review and any **Editorial hold** is cleared, publish it once.
4. Verify that the public schedule displays the approved dates, venue names, and registration links. Then record the published version and publication time.

Direct questions this procedure leaves unresolved to the Schedule procedure owner.
