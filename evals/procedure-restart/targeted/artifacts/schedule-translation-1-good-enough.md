# Publish regional workshop schedules

Use the translation workbook approved by Localization. Before building the draft, confirm that its **Workbook version** field matches the approval record.

1. Run **Build schedule** with the approved workbook.
2. In **Review draft**, compare the dates, venue names, and registration links against the workbook. Follow the hold instructions below if you find disputed wording or a broken registration link. Keep the last published schedule live until the replacement passes review.
3. Once the draft passes review and all holds are cleared, publish it once.
4. Check that the public schedule displays the approved dates, venue names, and registration links. After confirming all three, record the workbook version and publication time.

## Resolve issues found in Review draft

- **Disputed wording.** Set **Editorial hold** and leave the current schedule live. Send the disputed phrase and workbook version to the Regional editor, who owns the wording decision. Keep the hold until the Regional editor confirms the disposition in writing. Retain the confirmation with the workbook. Ask the Schedule procedure owner where to resume after the hold clears.
- **Broken registration link.** Leave the draft unpublished and notify the Registration team, which owns approved registration URLs. When Registration supplies the approved URL, replace the link in the draft. Resume at **Review draft** and repeat all three comparisons.
- **Both issues.** Keep **Editorial hold** until the Regional editor confirms the wording disposition in writing, even if Registration has supplied the approved URL. Ask the Schedule procedure owner how to sequence the link replacement, hold clearance, and renewed review.
