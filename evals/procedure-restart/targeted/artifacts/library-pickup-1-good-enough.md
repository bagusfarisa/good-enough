# Prepare library pickup holds

Start with the current reservation list. If a reservation expires before you send the pickup notice, stop preparation and follow the expiration procedure below.

1. **Check the item barcode.** Confirm that it matches the reservation. If you cannot locate the item after printing its pickup label, follow the missing-item procedure below.
2. **Shelve the item.** Place the matched item for an active reservation on the pickup shelf and scan the shelf slot in the pickup system.
3. **Verify the slot and send the notice.** Confirm that the scanned slot appears in the reservation record and that the reservation is still active. Then send the standard pickup notice.

## Item missing after label printing

1. Pause the reservation, keep the printed label at the desk, and set the reservation to **Search pending**.
2. Ask the Collections desk to investigate. Collections decides whether a replacement is available.
3. Once Collections confirms a replacement and the reservation is cleared to proceed, restart at step 1 using the confirmed replacement barcode. Keep the old label at the desk until the new shelf slot appears in the reservation record.

## Reservation expires before notice

1. Stop preparation, set the reservation to **Expired review**, and keep any item behind the desk until Member Services responds. Do not send a pickup notice while the reservation is expired.
2. Ask Member Services to decide whether the reservation can be reinstated. If it is reinstated, ask the Circulation process owner where to restart.
3. If the reservation expires during an open search, ask the Circulation process owner how to handle the pending Collections search.

## End-of-shift handoff

Hand off every open search or review with the reservation ID and its recorded owner. Refer any other unresolved exceptions to the Circulation process owner.
