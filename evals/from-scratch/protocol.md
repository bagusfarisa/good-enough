# Creation pilot protocol

Prepared before generation. Three synthetic briefs cover a Markdown decision memo, an editable XLSX, and an editable PPTX. Each runs three times under a plain prompt, the current skill, and the candidate with an added creation section. All conditions have the same task, source, output format, and tool access.

Publish the candidate only if all nine candidate artifacts pass source fidelity and applicable file checks, and it improves at least two formats over the current skill. Improvement means more complete usable artifacts, or fewer judge-identified unnecessary structural units with equal or better usability. No format may lose usability. Compare the plain prompt to understand the model's unaided performance.

Score source fidelity, artifact usability, reader answers, and reader citations separately. Count sections, visible tabs, and slides as descriptive measures. Judge whether a specific removable or mergeable unit adds friction while holding evidence and scope fixed. Measure workbook behavior after editing the actual input. Inspect rendered decks separately. Score usability using what a reader can see, excluding speaker notes. Keep reader misses distinct from content flaws and use the results as a small automated pilot.
