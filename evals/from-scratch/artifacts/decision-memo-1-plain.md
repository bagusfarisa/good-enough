# Decision memo: Renewals intake staffing

**To:** Operations Director  
**For:** Monday’s staffing meeting

## Decision requested

Reserve up to **$6,400** for one four-week intake pilot at North Desk. Continue manual triage until the written data retention plan is in place. Authorize the queue launch separately once that requirement is met. A second desk is outside the scope of this decision.

## Why reserve the pilot budget

Renewals missed the one-business-day response target on **12 of 40 requests (30%)** over the last four weeks. North Desk can run the new intake queue with its existing shift coverage and is available to start June 8. The start date depends on the written data retention plan. The plan owner and clearance date are still unconfirmed.

The alternative is to extend manual triage. That requires no pilot spending and keeps the current handoff in place.

## Four-week review

At the end of the pilot, review the number and share of requests that missed the response target against the baseline of 12 of 40. Use the same one-business-day definition for both periods.
