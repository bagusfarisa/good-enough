# Decision memo for Monday’s staffing meeting

**To:** Operations Director  
**Decision:** Whether to reserve up to **$6,400** for one four-week renewals intake pilot at North Desk.

## Recommendation

Reserve the pilot budget on Monday and make launch conditional on a written data retention plan. Assign a plan owner and confirm a clearance date. Keep manual triage in place until the launch requirement is met.

## Basis for the decision

Renewals missed the one-business-day response target on **12 of 40 requests (30%)** over the last four weeks. North Desk can run the new intake queue as early as **June 8** with its existing shift coverage. No owner or clearance date has been confirmed for the written retention plan, so June 8 remains conditional.

The alternative is to extend manual triage. It requires no pilot spend and leaves the current handoff in place. A second desk is outside the scope of Monday’s decision.

## Review after four weeks

At the end of the pilot, review the count and share of missed responses using the same one-business-day definition. Compare the results with the baseline of **12 misses out of 40 requests**.
