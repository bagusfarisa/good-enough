# Options paper for May warehouse dispatch pilot

## Origin

Dispatch can currently miss a route assignment when the queue changes close to shift handover. This is a decision paper for the COO. The working group weighed two possible routes to a pilot and leaving the current process in place. Any pilot is limited to the North warehouse, weekday morning shift, for two weeks. The current manual review stays available as the fallback during a pilot.

## Options discussion

Atlas can start May 18 and costs $5,500 for the pilot. It exports a timestamped audit log. Beacon costs $3,200. Beacon cannot export the required audit log until June 8, so Beacon cannot meet the May 18 start requirement. Keeping the manual process avoids a pilot fee but leaves the warehouse without the proposed routing check. The working group recommends Atlas for the limited pilot if both launch gates clear.

## Evaluation plan

After the two weeks, compare missed assignments with the two preceding weeks. Continue only if missed assignments fall by at least 20% and the audit log has no gaps. The COO must approve a broader rollout as a separate decision. Shift leaders keep the manual review in place until the pilot starts.

## Assurance review

Priya from Safety will approve or reject the manual override drill by May 14 at 12:00 UTC. The test must show that a shift leader can restore the manual dispatch queue within 10 minutes. A failed or late drill means the warehouse continues manual dispatch, even if the scanner check passes.

## Systems review

Luis from Integrations will approve or reject the East-facing scanner feed test by May 15 at 16:00 UTC. The test must show that every scan in the sample reaches Atlas once. A failed or late scanner test means the warehouse continues manual dispatch, even if the drill passes. No approval is implicit when either owner has missed the deadline.

## Material for the decision meeting

Confirm whether the COO will reserve up to $5,500 for Atlas. Reserving the funds does not authorize launch before the gates clear. The next checkpoint will cover the override drill, the scanner feed test, cost, audit coverage, and decisions after the pilot. The group will use the normal decision register to record approval.
