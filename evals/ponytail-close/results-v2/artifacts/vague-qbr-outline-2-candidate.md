# Quarterly business review deck outline

Use this deck to agree with the customer on next quarter’s priorities, owners, and deadlines. Replace bracketed text with account-specific information. Write each slide title as a takeaway once the evidence supports it.

| Slide | Suggested title | What to include |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **This quarter’s result and the decision for next quarter** | Name the customer’s top objective, the most important result, and the decision you need from the room. Put the decision owner and deadline beside the ask. |
| 2 | **We met [goal] and are behind on [goal]** | Use one row per agreed customer goal. Show the baseline, quarterly target, actual result, and source or measurement period. Flag any metric that could change the decision and still needs validation. |
| 3 | **[Workflow or team] drove the biggest change** | Show where adoption or usage changed and how it relates to the customer’s goals. Use the relevant teams or workflows and include denominators where they matter. Label estimated business impact and state the assumption behind it. |
| 4 | **[Blocker] puts [customer goal] at risk** | Show the impact of each material blocker, the mitigation, and its owner and due date. Include the specific customer action or decision needed, if any. Fold this into slide 3 if there is only one blocker and it has a straightforward fix. |
| 5 | **Next quarter’s plan focuses on [priority]** | Propose two or three actions tied to customer outcomes. For each, show the success measure and target, accountable owner, and milestone date. Close by confirming decisions, outstanding approvals, and the next check-in. |

*Assumption: This is a customer-facing review. Bring in renewal, expansion, or roadmap details when they affect a decision or commitment in the meeting.*
