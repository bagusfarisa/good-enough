# Quarterly business review deck outline

Use this six-slide outline for a customer-facing QBR. The account manager’s goal is to agree with the customer on what to prioritize next quarter, who owns each action, and how both teams will measure progress.

| Slide | Takeaway title to adapt | What to show |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Quarter at a glance | **[Outcome] improved, and [priority] needs attention next quarter** | State the customer’s main objective, the quarter’s most important result, and the decision or support you need in this meeting. If the outcome is still unmeasured, say what you can verify. |
| 2. Results against goals | **[Goal] met its target, while [goal] is still behind** | Show each agreed goal with its baseline, quarter-end result, and target. Label the reporting period and source. Where only usage is available, label it as an adoption measure and identify the business outcome still to be measured. |
| 3. Adoption and opportunities | **[Team or workflow] drove adoption, and [group] has room to expand** | Use one chart or a short table to show where usage changed. Explain what the pattern means for the customer’s goals and identify the next opportunity it supports. |
| 4. Risks and dependencies | **[Blocker] puts [customer goal] at risk** | For each material issue, show the impact, current status, owner, and next action with a date. Put any decision or support needed from the customer beside the issue. |
| 5. Next-quarter plan | **We propose [priority] to reach [target] by [date]** | Tie each proposed priority to a customer goal. Show the success measure, target, owner, and first milestone. Put any approval or dependency beside the action it affects. |
| 6. Decisions and commitments | **Today we need agreement on [specific decision]** | Confirm what was agreed, who owns each follow-up, and when it is due. For an open decision, record the decision owner and deadline. Set the next check-in. |

Omit slide 4 if there are no material issues. Keep detailed usage, support history, and metric definitions in an appendix when the customer needs them for verification.
