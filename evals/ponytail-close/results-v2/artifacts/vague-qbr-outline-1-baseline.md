# Quarterly business review deck outline

**Audience** — Customer sponsor and operational leads  
**Presenter** — Account manager  
**Meeting outcome** — Agree on next quarter’s priorities, owners, and success measures.

| Slide | Working title | What to include |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Quarter at a glance and decisions needed** | Open with the most important customer outcome, the biggest gap, and the decision you need today. Put your recommendation and any approval deadline beside the ask. |
| 2 | **Progress against agreed goals** | For each goal, show the baseline, quarterly target, actual result, and what it means for the customer. Include the reporting period and source. If a business outcome is unmeasured, label any usage metric you show as a proxy. |
| 3 | **Where adoption helped or stalled** | Show which teams or workflows gained traction and where adoption fell short. Use a trend or team comparison if it helps explain the result. Identify the blocker and the action that would address it. |
| 4 | **Open issues that could affect next quarter** | Carry forward unresolved commitments that could affect outcomes. For each, show the business impact, owner, and next milestone. Include an upcoming renewal or dependency here if it changes what the customer needs to decide now. |
| 5 | **Proposed priorities for next quarter** | Present up to three priorities. For each, specify the desired outcome, first action, customer or vendor owner, due date, and success measure. Flag any approval or dependency that could change the plan. |
| 6 | **Decisions and commitments** | Confirm what was approved, what remains open, and who will do what by when. Set the next checkpoint. For any deferred decision, record the decision owner and the date it’s needed. |

Use takeaway titles once the account data is in. For example, replace “Where adoption helped or stalled” with “Support adoption reached 80%, and onboarding is the next rollout priority” when the evidence supports both points.
