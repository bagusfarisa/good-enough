# Quarterly business review deck outline

Use this customer-facing deck to agree on next quarter’s priorities, success measures, and owners. Replace bracketed text with account-specific evidence.

| Slide | Takeaway title | What to show |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Customer] achieved [main result]. Next quarter, we recommend [priority] | Open with the most important result against an agreed goal. State the decision you need today and who needs to make it. |
| 2 | [X of Y] commitments are complete | Show each prior commitment, its status, and its result. For any carryover, state what blocked it and whether it needs a new owner or date. |
| 3 | [Business outcome] reached [actual] against a target of [target] | For each agreed outcome, show the baseline, target, actual, reporting period, and source. If business impact hasn’t been measured, label any usage metric as a proxy and name what would verify the impact. |
| 4 | [Team or workflow] is the biggest opportunity to improve adoption | Show where adoption is strong and where it’s lagging. Use a trend or a team comparison with a clear denominator. Connect the gap to a customer goal and flag any suspected cause that needs validation. |
| 5 | [Blocker] needs [action] by [date] | Include only issues that could change the plan, such as an unresolved integration or support issue. Show the impact, proposed action, owner, and due date. If a renewal or expansion decision is due next quarter, include the milestone and who needs to act. |
| 6 | We’ll deliver [next-quarter priorities] and review progress on [date] | Agree on up to three priorities. For each, record the success measure, accountable owner, and due date. Confirm decisions in the meeting and assign an owner and deadline to anything still open. |

The outline assumes a customer-facing review. Account results, targets, and renewal dates are placeholders until the account team verifies them.
