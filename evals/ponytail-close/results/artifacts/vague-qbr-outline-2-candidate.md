# Quarterly Business Review deck outline

Use this seven-slide outline for a customer-facing QBR. By the end of the meeting, the account manager and customer should agree on what to prioritize next quarter, who owns each action, and how they’ll measure success.

| Slide | What to show |
|---|---|
| **1. Quarter in review and decision needed** | Name the customer’s top objective, summarize the quarter’s most important result, and state the decision you need today. Put any deadline or dependency beside the decision. |
| **2. Progress against agreed goals** | For each priority, show the metric, baseline, quarterly target, and actual result. Explain material gaps in one line. Label product usage as a proxy when the business outcome hasn’t been measured. |
| **3. Where adoption is helping or stalling** | Show the one or two usage trends that affect the customer’s goals. Break them down by team or workflow only if it changes the next action. Include the period and denominator for each rate. |
| **4. Open issues and recovery actions** | Cover issues that could affect next quarter’s goals. For each, show the customer impact, current status, owner, and next milestone. Omit this slide if there are no material open issues. |
| **5. Priorities for next quarter** | Propose up to three priorities. For each, show the customer outcome, the first action, the measure of success, and any dependency. Mark proposals that still need customer approval. |
| **6. Decisions and commitments** | Confirm each decision and capture the agreed action, named owner, and due date. For anything unresolved, record who will decide and by when. |
| **7. Next checkpoint** | Confirm the date, attendees, and the evidence you’ll review to determine whether the plan is on track. Combine this with slide 6 when a single checkpoint covers the plan. |

The outline assumes an existing customer with agreed goals and access to usage data. Add a commercial slide when a renewal, expansion, or contract decision is on the agenda.
