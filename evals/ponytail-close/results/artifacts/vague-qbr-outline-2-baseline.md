# Quarterly business review deck outline

**Audience:** Customer sponsor and team leads  
**Presenter:** Account manager  
**Meeting goal:** Agree on what the customer wants to achieve next quarter, who owns each action, and what decisions are needed.

| Slide | Takeaway title to adapt | What to show |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Executive summary | **[Outcome] improved this quarter. Next quarter’s priority is [priority].** | Lead with the most important result, the biggest open risk, and the decision you need today. If a decision has a deadline, include it here. |
| 2. Results against goals | **We met [goal] and fell short on [goal].** | Use a compact scorecard with one row per agreed outcome. Show the baseline, quarter target, actual, and what the result means for the customer. Label estimates and flag outcomes you could not measure. |
| 3. Adoption and blockers | **[Team or workflow] drove progress. [Blocker] limits further gains.** | Show the usage trend or team comparison that helps explain the results. Identify the blocker, who it affects, and what would resolve it. Include support issues only when they affect an outcome or require action. |
| 4. Next-quarter plan | **We propose [one or two priorities] to achieve [customer objective].** | For each priority, name the success measure, target, owner, and due date. Put dependencies beside the actions they affect. If the plan depends on sponsor approval, state the decision and its deadline. |
| 5. Decisions and commitments | **Today we agreed to [decision or commitment].** | Update this slide live. Record each decision and action with an owner and due date. For any deferred decision, capture who will make it and by when. Confirm the next check-in. |

Use the customer’s wording for goals and keep the reporting period and source beside each key metric. Add a renewal or expansion slide only when there’s a commercial decision to discuss. Keep detailed usage and support data in an appendix if attendees need it for follow-up.
