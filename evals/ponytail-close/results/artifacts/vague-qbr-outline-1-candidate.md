# Quarterly business review deck outline

Use this customer-facing deck to agree on next quarter’s priorities, owners, and success measures. Put the decision you need from the customer on slide 1, then close by confirming who will do what and by when. Replace bracketed text with account-specific evidence.

| Slide | Takeaway title | What to include |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Executive summary | **[Customer] achieved [result]. Next quarter, we recommend [priority].** | Name the customer and review period. Give the most important result, the biggest gap, and the decision you need in the meeting. If the outcome is unmeasured, lead with the strongest verified result and name the measurement gap. |
| 2. Progress against goals | **[Goal] is on track. [Goal] needs attention.** | Show each agreed goal with its baseline, quarterly target, actual result, and status. Include the measurement period and source. Flag any target or baseline that still needs agreement. |
| 3. Adoption and impact | **[Team or workflow] drove [observed result].** | Show which teams or workflows adopted the product, where adoption stalled, and what changed for the customer. Label estimates and explain their basis. If you only have usage data, describe what it shows and identify the business outcome to measure next. |
| 4. Risks and decisions | **[Blocker] puts [customer goal] at risk.** | Cover only issues that need action or could change the plan. For each, show the impact, proposed response, owner, and decision date. Put any customer approval or dependency beside the affected action. |
| 5. Next-quarter plan | **We’ll focus on [priority] and measure [outcome].** | Agree on up to three priorities. For each, record the first action, accountable owner, due date, and success measure. Close by confirming decisions, any unresolved items, and the next checkpoint. |

Before presenting, check that the opening recommendation follows from the evidence and that every proposed action has an owner, a date, and a way to tell whether it worked.

This outline assumes a joint review with customer stakeholders. Add renewal or expansion content when it calls for a customer decision.
