# Loaner laptop return SOP

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Verify the asset tag.** Scan the tag and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake and the loan open until the discrepancy is resolved. Stop intake processing.
2. **Check the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   - **Visible battery swelling:** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities at extension **221**. Record the asset tag, cabinet location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears it.
   - **Surface scratches only:** Photograph the scratches and continue.
3. **Acknowledge receipt** when the tag matches and there is no visible battery swelling.
   - **Charger missing:** Set the status to **Accessories pending** and record the missing charger in the asset register. The laptop can go through normal cleanup. Keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery.
   - **Return complete:** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Wait for **Reset successful** before setting the status to **Returned**, recording the receipt timestamp, and placing the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Review the daily return list. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold update to the existing record. Use the existing asset register and incident log, and keep one loan record per return.
