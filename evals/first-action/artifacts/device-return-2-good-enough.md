# Loaner laptop return SOP

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

Scan the laptop’s asset tag and compare it with the loan record. If the tag differs, set the status to **Check required** and refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk. Keep the laptop in intake and the loan open until the Asset Desk resolves the discrepancy. If you see battery swelling, follow the safety hold steps below.

For a matching tag, check whether the charger is present and inspect the laptop for damage before powering it on.

- **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities at extension **221**. Record the asset tag, cabinet location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears the hold.
- **Surface scratches only.** Photograph the scratches and continue with the return.

## Process the return

Once the tag matches and you have found no battery swelling, give the borrower an acknowledgment.

- **Charger missing.** Set the status to **Accessories pending** and record the missing charger in the asset register. You can proceed with normal laptop cleanup. Keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges charger recovery.
- **Laptop and charger received.** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Wait for the tool to display **Reset successful**. Then set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## End-of-day review

At close, review the daily return list and verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach hold updates to the existing record. Use the existing asset register and incident log, and keep one loan record per return.
