# Loaner laptop return SOP

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Check for visible battery swelling before powering on or charging the laptop.** If you see swelling, move the laptop to the designated battery isolation cabinet. Set the status to **Safety hold**, call Facilities on extension **221**, and record the asset tag, cabinet location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears the hold. Stop ordinary processing.
2. **Scan the asset tag and compare it with the loan record.** If it differs, set the status to **Check required** and refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk. Keep the laptop in intake and the loan open until the Asset Desk resolves the discrepancy.
3. **For a matching tag, check for the charger and inspect the laptop for damage before powering it on.** Photograph surface scratches and continue if they are the only damage. Give the borrower an acknowledgment of receipt.
4. **If the charger is missing,** set the status to **Accessories pending**, record the missing charger in the asset register, and keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery. The laptop can proceed through normal cleanup.

## Close a complete return

1. Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Wait for **Reset successful** before closing the loan.
2. Set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp in the asset register, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At the end of the day

Check the daily return list. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold updates to the existing record and use the existing incident log for safety incidents. Keep one loan record per return.
