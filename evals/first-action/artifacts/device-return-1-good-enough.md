# Loaner laptop returns at intake

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger.
3. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

Check for visible battery swelling before applying the asset-tag and charger checks. Keep the laptop powered off during inspection.

| What you find | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Visible battery swelling, regardless of tag match** | Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities at extension **221**. Record the asset tag, location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears it. If the tag also differs, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk and wait for resolution before closing the loan. |
| **Asset tag differs, with no visible battery swelling** | Stop processing the return. Set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake and the loan open until the discrepancy is resolved. |
| **Asset tag matches, with no visible battery swelling** | Check for the charger and finish inspecting the laptop for damage before powering it on. If the only damage is surface scratches, photograph them and continue. Give the borrower an acknowledgment. |

## Process an ordinary return

- **Charger missing.** Set the status to **Accessories pending** and note the missing charger in the asset register. Keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery. The laptop can go through normal cleanup.
- **Laptop and charger received.** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Once the tool displays **Reset successful**, set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Review the daily return list. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold updates to the existing record. Use the existing asset register and incident log, and keep one loan record per return.
