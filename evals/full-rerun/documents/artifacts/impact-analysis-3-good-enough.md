# Return request expiry at launch

After launch, return requests expire after **seven calendar days in Awaiting customer documents**. Today, open requests stay open indefinitely unless someone closes them. The seven-day clock starts when a request enters that status.

**Product needs to decide whether the rule applies to requests already open on launch day.** Support and Operations need that decision before they can tell which existing requests can expire under the new rule. Communications still needs to decide whether customers will receive an expiry email.

| Case | Current behavior | After launch | Team action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awaiting customer documents, in scope | Stays open until someone closes it. | Expires after seven calendar days in the status. The portal shows **Expired**. The expired request stays closed, and the customer can submit a new request. | Support explains how to submit a new request. Operations uses the date the request entered the status to determine its expiry date. |
| Already open on launch day | Stays open until someone closes it. | Whether the rule applies to existing requests is still open. | Product confirms which existing requests are in scope so Support and Operations can apply the rule consistently. |
| Internal review | Stays open until someone closes it. | Stays open until someone closes it. | Operations continues the existing review process. |
| Completed or cancelled | Already closed. | Remains in its current status. | No action needed. |

**Example.** R-82 was created nine days ago and entered Awaiting customer documents two days ago. If it is in scope, it has **five calendar days left** in that status.
