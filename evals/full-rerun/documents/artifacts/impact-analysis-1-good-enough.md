# Return request expiry

After launch, in-scope return requests will expire after seven calendar days in **Awaiting customer documents**. The clock starts when the request enters that status. The customer will see **Expired** in the portal and can submit a new request. The expired request stays closed.

**Product needs to decide whether the rule applies to requests already open on launch day.** Until Product confirms the scope, Support and Operations should treat expiry dates for those requests as conditional. Communications still needs to decide whether customers will receive an expiry email.

## Impact by request status

| Request status | Today | After launch |
|---|---|---|
| Awaiting customer documents | The request stays open until someone closes it. | In-scope requests expire after seven calendar days in this status. |
| Internal review | The request stays open until someone closes it. | The expiry rule does not apply. |
| Completed or cancelled | The record is already closed. | The record is unaffected. |

**Example:** R-82 was created nine days ago and entered **Awaiting customer documents** two days ago. If the rule applies to R-82, it has five calendar days left before expiry.

## Launch actions

| Owner | Action |
|---|---|
| Support | Explain what **Expired** means and walk customers through submitting a new return request. |
| Operations | Use the date the request entered **Awaiting customer documents** to determine its expiry date. Confirm launch-day scope before applying the rule to requests already open at launch. |
| Product | Decide whether requests already open on launch day are in scope and share the decision with Support and Operations. |
| Communications | Decide whether to send an expiry email. |
