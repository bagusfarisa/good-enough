# Return request expiry — launch impact analysis

After launch, a return request expires after seven calendar days in **Awaiting customer documents**. Today, open requests stay open indefinitely unless someone closes them. The seven-day clock starts when the request enters that status.

Customers will see **Expired** in the portal. They can submit a new return request, and the expired request stays closed.

## Impact by team

| Team | Impact |
|---|---|
| Support | Explain that the deadline runs from when the request entered **Awaiting customer documents**. When a customer sees **Expired**, guide them through submitting a new request. |
| Operations | Account for automatic closure of requests that reach seven calendar days in **Awaiting customer documents**. Requests in **Internal review** are unaffected. **Completed** and **Cancelled** records remain untouched. |
| Product | Decide whether the expiry rule applies to requests already open on launch day. That decision determines whether those requests can expire under the new rule. |

## Launch decisions

| Owner | Open decision |
|---|---|
| Product | Will the rule apply to requests that are already open on launch day? |
| Communications | Will customers receive an email when a request expires? The portal will show **Expired** regardless of the email decision. |

## Example for the launch discussion

R-82 was created nine days ago and entered **Awaiting customer documents** two days ago. If R-82 is in scope, it has **five calendar days left** in that status before it expires.
