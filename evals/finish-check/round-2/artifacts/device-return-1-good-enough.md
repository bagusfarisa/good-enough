# Loaner laptop return SOP

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger.
3. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Verify the asset tag.** Scan it and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, stop processing the return, set the status to **Check required**, and refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk. Keep the laptop in intake and the loan open. Once the Asset Desk resolves the discrepancy and confirms the correct record, continue at step 2.
   
   **If you see battery swelling and the tag also differs**, keep the laptop off and uncharged, and call Facilities on extension 221. Flag both issues to Facilities and the Asset Desk. The procedure owner must confirm which status and storage location take precedence in this case.

2. **Check the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   - **Visible battery swelling:** Keep the laptop off and uncharged. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities on extension 221. Record the tag, location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open. After Facilities clears the hold, return to step 2 and follow any handling instructions from Facilities.
   - **Surface scratches only:** Photograph the scratches and continue to step 3.

3. **Acknowledge receipt.** Give the borrower an acknowledgment once the tag matches and the laptop has no visible battery swelling.
   - **Charger missing:** Set the status to **Accessories pending**, note the missing charger in the asset register, and keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery. You can run the reset in step 4 while recovery is pending. Once the charger is recovered, continue at step 4 and use the successful reset result if cleanup is already complete.

4. **Reset and close the return.** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool and verify that it displays **Reset successful**. Once the return is complete and the reset has succeeded, set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Review the daily return list. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach hold updates to the existing record and use the existing incident log and asset register. Keep one loan record per return.
