# May warehouse dispatch pilot approval brief

## Decision requested

**Reserve up to $4,800 for Atlas. Authorize the May 18 launch only if both launch gates receive explicit approval by their deadlines.** If either gate fails or misses its deadline, continue manual dispatch.

The working group recommends Atlas to test whether a routing check reduces missed route assignments when the queue changes close to shift handover.
