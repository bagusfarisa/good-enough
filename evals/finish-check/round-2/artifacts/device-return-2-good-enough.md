# Loaner laptop return SOP

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Verify the asset tag.** Scan the tag and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, stop the ordinary return. Set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake and the loan open. Once the Asset Desk resolves the discrepancy, repeat step 1 using the confirmed record.
2. **Check the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities on extension **221**. Record the tag, cabinet location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears the hold. After clearance, repeat step 2 before continuing to step 3.
   - **Surface scratches alone.** Photograph the scratches and continue to step 3.
   - **Tag mismatch and visible swelling together.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Call Facilities on extension **221** and notify the Asset Desk. The procedure owner must confirm which location and status to use when both exceptions apply. Keep the loan open and resume at step 1 after both teams resolve their holds.
3. **Acknowledge receipt.** Give the borrower an acknowledgment once the tag matches and the laptop has no visible battery swelling. If the charger is missing, set the status to **Accessories pending**, record the missing charger in the asset register, and keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery. You may perform the reset in step 4 while recovery is pending. Once the Asset Desk confirms recovery, resume at step 4.
4. **Reset the laptop and close the return.** Use the approved reset tool to clear the borrower profile and verify that it displays **Reset successful**. Once the return is complete and the reset has succeeded, set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Check the daily return list. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold updates to the existing record and use the existing incident log and asset register. Keep one loan record per return.
