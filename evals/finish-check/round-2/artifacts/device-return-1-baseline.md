# Loaner laptop return SOP

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register and check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger.
3. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. Scan the laptop’s asset tag and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, stop the ordinary return. Set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake and the loan open pending resolution. If you see battery swelling, follow the safety hold steps below.
2. If the tag matches, check whether the charger is present and inspect the laptop for damage before powering it on.
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities on extension **221**. Record the asset tag, cabinet location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears it.
   - **Surface scratches only.** Photograph the scratches and continue.
3. For a matching tag with no visible battery swelling, give the borrower an acknowledgment.
   - **Charger missing.** Set the status to **Accessories pending** and record the missing charger in the asset register. Keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery. The laptop may go through normal cleanup.
   - **Charger present.** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Wait for **Reset successful** before setting the status to **Returned**, recording the receipt timestamp, and placing the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Check the daily return list. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold update to the existing record. Use the existing asset register and incident log, and keep a single loan record for each return.
