# Loaner laptop return SOP

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Confirm the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Match the asset tag.** Scan the laptop’s tag and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, stop the return. Set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake and the loan open pending resolution.
2. **Check the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities at extension **221**. Record the tag, location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears it.
   - **Surface scratches only.** Photograph the scratches and continue.
3. **Acknowledge the return** to the borrower once the tag matches and you have confirmed there is no visible battery swelling.
4. **Record a missing charger.** Set the status to **Accessories pending**, note the missing charger in the asset register, and keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery. The laptop can still go through normal cleanup.
5. **Close a complete return.** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Wait for **Reset successful**, then set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Review the daily return list and confirm that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold updates to the existing record. Use the existing asset register and incident log, and keep one loan record per return.
