# Loaner laptop return SOP

Set a loan to **Returned** only after you verify that the asset tag matches, the charger is present, the laptop shows no visible battery swelling, and the approved reset tool displays **Reset successful**.

## Before the appointment

- Find the loan in the asset register and check the recorded asset tag and due date.
- Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger.
- Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Scan the asset tag and compare it with the loan record.** If it differs, set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake. Stop processing and leave the loan open until the discrepancy is resolved.
2. **Check for the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities on extension **221**. Record the asset tag, location, and time in the incident log. Stop processing and leave the loan open until Facilities clears it.
   - **Surface scratches alone.** Photograph the scratches and continue.
3. **Give the borrower an acknowledgment** once the tag matches and you have confirmed there is no visible battery swelling. If the charger is missing, set the status to **Accessories pending** and record the missing charger in the asset register. You can continue normal laptop cleanup. Keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery.
4. **Complete the return once the charger is present.** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Wait for **Reset successful**, then set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp in the asset register, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Review the daily return list. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold updates to the existing record. Use the existing asset register and incident log, and keep one loan record per return.
