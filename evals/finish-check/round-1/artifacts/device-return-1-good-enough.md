# Loaner laptop returns at intake

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register and check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Verify the asset tag.** Scan the tag and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, stop intake, set the status to `Check required`, and refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk. Keep the laptop in intake and leave the loan open. Once the Asset Desk resolves the discrepancy, repeat step 1 before continuing.
   
   **If the tag differs and you also see battery swelling**, the procedure does not specify which hold or location takes precedence. Keep the loan open, do not power on or charge the laptop, and contact Facilities on extension 221 and the Asset Desk for handling instructions.

2. **Check the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to `Safety hold`, and call Facilities on extension 221. Record the tag, cabinet location, and time in the incident log. Leave the loan open until Facilities clears it. Attach the clearance to the existing record and follow Facilities’ handling instructions before resuming at step 2.
   - **Surface scratches only.** Photograph the scratches and continue to step 3.

3. **Acknowledge receipt to the borrower.** If the charger is missing, set the status to `Accessories pending`, note the missing charger in the asset register, and leave the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery. You can perform the reset in step 4 during the hold. Once the charger is recovered, resume at step 4 if the reset is still pending, or at step 5 if it already reported `Reset successful`.

4. **Clear the borrower profile.** Use the approved reset tool and wait for `Reset successful`. Keep the loan open until the reset succeeds.

5. **Close the return.** Once the charger is accounted for and the reset has reported `Reset successful`, set the status to `Returned`, record the receipt timestamp in the asset register, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Review the daily return list. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach hold updates to the existing record and use the existing incident log for safety details. Keep one loan record per return.
