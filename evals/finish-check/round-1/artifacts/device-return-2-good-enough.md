# Loaner laptop returns at intake

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Verify the asset tag.** Scan the tag and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake and the loan open. Once the Asset Desk resolves the discrepancy and confirms the correct loan record, resume at step 2.
   
   If you see battery swelling while the tag discrepancy is still open, do not power on or charge the laptop. Call Facilities on extension 221 and alert the Asset Desk. The procedure owner must clarify which location and status apply when both holds are active. Keep the loan open and pause intake until you receive direction.

2. **Check the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities on extension 221. Record the tag, cabinet location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears it. Attach the clearance to the existing record, then resume at step 3 once the laptop is cleared for normal handling.
   - **Surface scratches only.** Photograph the scratches and continue to step 3.

3. **Acknowledge receipt and record any missing charger.** Give the borrower an acknowledgment. If the charger is missing, set the status to **Accessories pending**, note the missing charger in the asset register, and keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery. You may perform the reset in step 4 while recovery is pending. Once the charger is recovered, resume at step 4 if the reset is outstanding or at step 5 if the reset already succeeded.

4. **Reset the laptop.** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Wait for **Reset successful** before proceeding to step 5. If the tool does not report success, keep the loan open.

5. **Close the return.** Once the charger is accounted for and the reset has succeeded, set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## End-of-day review

Check the daily return list at close. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold updates to the existing record. Use the existing asset register and incident log, and keep a single loan record for each return.
