# May warehouse dispatch pilot approval brief

**Decision requested.** Reserve up to **$4,800** for a two-week Atlas pilot starting **May 18**, limited to the **North warehouse weekday morning shift**. The working group recommends Atlas because it can meet the start date and export the required timestamped audit log.

**Launch condition.** The funding reservation is for the pilot. Launch requires explicit approval from both gate owners by their deadlines. If either test fails or approval is late or missing, continue manual dispatch.

## Launch gates

| Gate | Owner and deadline | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Manual override drill | Priya, Safety · May 14, 12:00 UTC | A shift leader restores the manual dispatch queue within 10 minutes. |
| East-facing scanner feed test | Luis, Integrations · May 15, 16:00 UTC | Every scan in the sample reaches Atlas exactly once. |

Shift leaders keep manual review in place until launch and retain it as the fallback during the pilot.

## Options

Dispatch can miss route assignments when the queue changes close to shift handover.

| Option | Pilot fee | Trade-off |
|---|---:|---|
| Atlas | $4,800 | Can start May 18 and export a timestamped audit log, subject to both launch gates. |
| Beacon | $3,200 | Can export the required audit log starting June 8, after the required May 18 start. |
| Continue manual dispatch | $0 | Avoids the pilot fee and leaves dispatch without the proposed routing check. |

## Follow-up and rollout decision

Record the COO’s funding decision in the normal decision register. At the next checkpoint, review both gate outcomes, cost, audit coverage, and the decisions due after the pilot.

After two weeks, compare missed assignments during the pilot with the two weeks immediately before it. Continue only if missed assignments fall by **at least 20%** and the audit log has **no gaps**. Any broader rollout requires separate COO approval.
