# Loaner laptop returns at the service desk

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger.
3. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Scan the asset tag and compare it with the loan record.** If it differs, stop the ordinary return. Set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake and the loan open while you wait for resolution.
2. **If the tag matches, check for the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities on extension **221**. Record the asset tag, cabinet location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears it.
   - **Surface scratches only.** Photograph the scratches and continue with the ordinary return.
3. **If the tag matches and there is no battery swelling, acknowledge receipt to the borrower.**
   - **Charger missing.** Set the status to **Accessories pending** and record the missing charger in the asset register. You may proceed with normal laptop cleanup. Keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery.
   - **Charger present.** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Wait for **Reset successful**, then set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

A complete ordinary return has a successful reset, a **Returned** status, a recorded receipt timestamp, and the laptop on the ready shelf.

## End-of-day review

1. Check the daily return list at close. Verify that each open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner.
2. Attach any hold update to the existing record. Use the asset register and incident log for the same return, and keep a single loan record.

## Process owner decisions

The process owner needs to specify which status and storage location apply when a tag mismatch and visible battery swelling occur together. The process owner also needs to specify where operators resume after a tag discrepancy is resolved, Facilities clears a safety hold, or the Asset Desk recovers a missing charger.
