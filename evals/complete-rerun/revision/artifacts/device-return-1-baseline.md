# Loaner laptop returns at intake

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger.
3. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Check the asset tag.** Scan it and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake and the loan open while you wait for resolution. Stop the ordinary return. If you see battery swelling, follow the safety steps below and leave the tag discrepancy with the Asset Desk.
2. **Check the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities at extension **221**. Record the asset tag, location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears the hold.
   - **Surface scratches only.** Photograph the scratches and continue.

## Complete the ordinary return

Give the borrower a return acknowledgment once the tag matches and you have confirmed there is no visible battery swelling.

- **Charger missing.** Set the status to **Accessories pending** and record the missing charger in the asset register. Keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery. The laptop can go through normal cleanup.
- **Laptop and charger received.** Use the approved reset tool to clear the borrower profile. Wait for **Reset successful**. Then set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Check the daily return list. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold updates to the existing loan record. Use the existing asset register and incident log, and keep a single loan record for each return.
