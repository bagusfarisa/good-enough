# Loaner laptop return SOP

**Close a complete return only after the approved reset tool displays `Reset successful`.** Keep the loan open while a tag discrepancy, safety hold, or missing charger is unresolved.

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger.
3. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Scan the asset tag and compare it with the loan record.** If it differs, stop the ordinary return. Set the status to `Check required`, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake and the loan open pending resolution. If you also see battery swelling, do not power on or charge the laptop. Escalate the overlap to Facilities on extension 221 and the Asset Desk. The process owner must confirm which location and status take precedence when both exceptions apply.
2. **For a matching tag, check for the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to `Safety hold`, and call Facilities on extension 221. Record the asset tag, location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears it.
   - **Surface scratches only.** Photograph the scratches and continue.
3. **For a matching tag with no battery swelling, give the borrower an acknowledgment.** If the charger is missing, set the status to `Accessories pending` and note the missing charger in the asset register. The Asset Desk arranges recovery. The laptop can proceed through normal cleanup while the loan stays open.

## Close a complete return

1. Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool.
2. Wait for `Reset successful`. Once it appears, set the status to `Returned`, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

The process owner must confirm where to resume after a tag discrepancy is resolved, Facilities clears a safety hold, or a missing charger is recovered.

## At close of day

- Review the daily return list. Verify that each open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner.
- Attach any hold update to the existing record. Use the existing asset register and incident log. Do not create a second loan record for the same return.
