# Loaner laptop return SOP

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register and check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger.
3. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Verify the asset tag.** Scan the tag and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, stop normal processing. Set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and keep the laptop in intake with the loan open while you wait for resolution. If you see battery swelling, follow the safety steps below.
2. **Check the charger and inspect the laptop.** For a matching tag, check whether the charger is present and inspect the laptop for damage before powering it on.
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities at extension **221**. Record the asset tag, cabinet location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears it.
   - **Surface scratches only.** Photograph the scratches and continue.
3. **Acknowledge the return.** Give the borrower an acknowledgment once the tag matches and you have checked for battery swelling.
   - **Charger missing.** Set the status to **Accessories pending** and record the missing charger in the asset register. The laptop can go through normal cleanup. Keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery.
   - **Laptop and charger received.** Clear the borrower profile using the approved reset tool. Wait for **Reset successful**, then set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Check the daily return list. Confirm that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold updates to the existing record. Keep one loan record per return and use the existing asset register and incident log.
