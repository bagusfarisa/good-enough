# Decision brief for the COO | May warehouse dispatch pilot

**Decision requested.** Reserve up to **$4,800 for Atlas** for a two-week pilot on the weekday morning shift at the North warehouse. The working group recommends Atlas because it can start May 18 and export the required timestamped audit log. Record the funding decision in the normal decision register.

**Launch condition.** Start May 18 only if both owners explicitly approve their tests by the deadlines below. If either test fails or its approval is late or missing, continue manual dispatch. Shift leaders keep manual review in place until launch and available as the fallback throughout the pilot.

| Gate | Owner | Approval deadline (UTC) | Pass criterion |

## After the pilot

Compare missed assignments during the two-week pilot with the two preceding weeks. Continue only if missed assignments fall by **at least 20%** and the audit log has **no gaps**. Any broader rollout requires a separate COO approval.

At the next checkpoint, review both launch gates, cost, audit coverage, and the decisions due after the pilot.
