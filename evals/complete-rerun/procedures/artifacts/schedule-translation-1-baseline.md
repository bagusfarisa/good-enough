# Publish regional workshop schedules

Keep the last published schedule live until the replacement passes review and any editorial hold is cleared.

## Prerequisite

Use the translation workbook approved by Localization. Confirm that the **Workbook version** field matches the approval record before building the draft.

## Procedure

1. Generate the draft with **Build schedule**.
2. In **Review draft**, compare the dates, venue names, and registration links against the workbook. Resolve any issues before publishing.

   | Review finding | Required action |
   | --- | --- |
   | Disputed wording | Set **Editorial hold**. Send the disputed phrase and workbook version to the Regional editor, who owns the wording decision. Keep the hold in place until the Regional editor confirms the disposition in writing. Retain the confirmation with the workbook. |
   | Broken registration link | Keep the draft unpublished and notify the Registration team, which owns approved registration URLs. When Registration supplies the approved URL, replace the link in the draft. Return to **Review draft** and repeat all three comparisons, using the approved URL for the corrected link. If wording is also disputed, keep **Editorial hold** in place until the Regional editor confirms the disposition in writing. |

3. Once the draft passes review and any **Editorial hold** is cleared, publish it once.
4. Open the public schedule and confirm that it displays the approved dates, venue names, and registration links. After this check passes, record the version and publication time.

Direct questions this procedure leaves unresolved to the Schedule procedure owner.
