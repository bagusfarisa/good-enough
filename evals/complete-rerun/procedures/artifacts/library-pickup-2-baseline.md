# Prepare library pickup holds

Have the current reservation list and access to the pickup system before you start.

1. Check that the item barcode matches the reservation and that the reservation is active. If the item is missing after you printed its pickup label, follow **Item missing after label printing**.
2. Place the matched item on the pickup shelf and scan the shelf slot in the pickup system.
3. Confirm that the scanned slot appears in the reservation record and that the reservation is still active. Then send the standard pickup notice. If the reservation expires at any point before you send the notice, follow **Reservation expires before notice**.

Preparation is complete when the correct shelf slot appears in the reservation record and you have sent the standard pickup notice.

## Item missing after label printing

1. Pause preparation, keep the printed label at the desk, and set the reservation status to **Search pending**.
2. Ask the Collections desk to investigate. Collections decides whether a replacement item is available.
3. When Collections confirms a replacement and the reservation is cleared to proceed, restart at step 1 using the confirmed replacement barcode. Keep the old label at the desk until the new shelf slot appears in the reservation record.

## Reservation expires before notice

1. Stop preparation and set the reservation status to **Expired review**. Do not send a pickup notice while the reservation is expired.
2. Ask Member Services whether the reservation can be reinstated. Keep any item behind the desk until Member Services responds.

## Shift handoff

Refer unresolved exceptions to the Circulation process owner. At the end of the shift, hand off every open search or review with its reservation ID and recorded owner.
