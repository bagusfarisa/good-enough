# Prepare library pickup holds

**Before you start:** Use the current reservation list.

## Prepare each hold

1. **Check the item barcode.** Verify that it matches the reservation.
2. **Shelve the item and scan the slot.** If the barcode matches and the reservation is active, place the item on the pickup shelf and scan its shelf slot in the pickup system.
3. **Verify the record and send the notice.** Confirm that the scanned slot appears in the reservation record and that the reservation is still active. Send the standard pickup notice. The hold is ready for pickup when the slot is recorded and the notice has been sent.

## If the item is missing after you print the label

Pause work on the reservation. Keep the printed label at the desk, set the reservation to **Search pending**, and ask the Collections desk to investigate. Collections decides whether a replacement is available.

When Collections confirms a replacement and the reservation is cleared to proceed, restart at step 1 using the confirmed replacement barcode. Keep the old label at the desk until the new shelf slot appears in the reservation record.

## If the reservation expires before you send the notice

Stop preparation and set the reservation to **Expired review**. Ask Member Services whether the reservation can be reinstated. Keep any item behind the desk until Member Services responds. Do not send a pickup notice while the reservation is expired.

Ask the Circulation process owner where to restart if Member Services reinstates the reservation. If a reservation expires during an open item search, stop preparation and ask the process owner how to handle the two open exceptions.

## End-of-shift handoff

Hand off every open search or review with its reservation ID and recorded owner. Refer any unresolved exception to the Circulation process owner.
