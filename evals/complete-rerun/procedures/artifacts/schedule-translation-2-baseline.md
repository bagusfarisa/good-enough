# Publish a regional workshop schedule

Keep the current schedule live until the replacement passes review.

## Prerequisite

Use the translation workbook approved by Localization. Confirm that its **Workbook version** field matches the approval record before building the draft.

## Build and review the draft

1. Run **Build schedule** to generate the draft.
2. In **Review draft**, compare the dates, venue names, and registration links against the approved workbook. Check that the registration links work.
3. Resolve any issues before publishing.

   | If review finds | What to do |
   | --- | --- |
   | Disputed wording | Set **Editorial hold**. Send the disputed phrase and workbook version to the Regional editor, who owns the wording decision. Keep the hold until the editor confirms the disposition in writing. Retain the confirmation with the workbook. |
   | A broken registration link | Keep the draft unpublished and notify the Registration team, which owns approved URLs. When Registration supplies the approved URL, replace the link in the draft. Return to **Review draft** and repeat all three comparisons, using Registration’s approved URL for the corrected link. Check that the corrected link works. |

   If both issues occur, keep **Editorial hold** in place until the Regional editor confirms the wording disposition in writing, even if the link has been fixed. Refer questions this procedure leaves unresolved to the Schedule procedure owner.

## Publish and verify

1. Publish the replacement once it passes review and any **Editorial hold** is cleared.
2. Open the public schedule and verify that it displays the approved dates, venue names, and registration links.
3. After the public check passes, record the published version and publication time.
