# Prepare library pickup holds

**Before you start:** Have the current reservation list and access to the pickup system.

1. Check that the item barcode matches the reservation and that the reservation is active.
2. Put the matched item on the pickup shelf and scan the shelf slot in the pickup system.
3. Confirm that the slot appears in the reservation record and that the reservation is still active. Then send the standard pickup notice.

## If the item is missing after you print the label

1. Pause preparation, keep the printed label at the desk, and set the reservation status to **Search pending**.
2. Ask the Collections desk to investigate. Collections decides whether a replacement is available.
3. Once Collections confirms a replacement and the reservation is cleared to proceed, restart at step 1 with the confirmed replacement barcode. Keep the old label at the desk until the new shelf slot appears in the reservation record.

## If the reservation expires before you send the notice

1. Stop preparation, set the reservation status to **Expired review**, and ask Member Services whether the reservation can be reinstated.
2. Keep any item behind the desk until Member Services responds. Do not send a pickup notice while the reservation is expired.

## Shift handoff

At the end of your shift, hand off every open search or review with the reservation ID and its recorded owner. Ask the Circulation process owner about unresolved exceptions.
