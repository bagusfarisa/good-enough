# Publish regional workshop schedules

**Prerequisite.** Use the translation workbook approved by Localization. Confirm that its **Workbook version** field matches the approval record before building the draft.

1. Run **Build schedule** to generate a draft. Keep the last published schedule live until the replacement passes review.
2. In **Review draft**, compare the dates, venue names, and registration links against the approved workbook. If you find disputed wording or a broken registration link, follow the instructions below before publishing.
3. Once the draft passes review and any holds are resolved, publish it once.
4. Check that the public schedule displays the approved dates, venue names, and registration links. Then record the workbook version and publication time.

## Resolve issues found in Review draft

- **Disputed wording.** Set **Editorial hold** and send the disputed phrase and workbook version to the Regional editor, who owns the wording decision. Keep the hold in place until the editor confirms the disposition in writing. Retain the confirmation with the workbook. If disputed wording is the only issue, ask the Schedule procedure owner where to resume after confirmation.
- **Broken registration link.** Keep the draft unpublished and notify the Registration team, which owns approved registration URLs. When Registration supplies the approved URL, replace the link in the draft. Resume at **Review draft** and repeat all three comparisons. If wording is also disputed, keep **Editorial hold** in place until the Regional editor confirms the wording disposition in writing.

Direct any other unresolved procedure questions to the Schedule procedure owner.
