# Prepare library pickup holds

**Before you start:** Have the current reservation list available.

1. Check that the item barcode matches the reservation. Confirm the reservation is active before shelving the item.
2. Put the matched item on the pickup shelf and scan the shelf slot in the pickup system.
3. Verify that the scanned slot appears in the reservation record. If the reservation is still active, send the standard pickup notice. The hold is ready when the slot is recorded and the notice has been sent.

## If the item is missing after you print the pickup label

Pause preparation for that reservation. Keep the printed label at the desk, set the reservation status to **Search pending**, and ask the Collections desk to investigate. Collections decides whether a replacement is available.

Once Collections confirms a replacement and the reservation is cleared to proceed, restart at step 1 using the confirmed replacement barcode. Keep the old label at the desk until the new shelf slot is recorded.

## If the reservation expires before you send the notice

Stop preparation and set the reservation status to **Expired review**. Ask Member Services whether the reservation can be reinstated. Keep any item behind the desk until Member Services responds. Do not send a pickup notice for an expired reservation.

If Member Services reinstates the reservation, ask the Circulation process owner where to restart. If a reservation expires during an open item search, follow the expiration steps above and ask the process owner how to handle the open search.

## End-of-shift handoff

Hand off every open search or review with its reservation ID and recorded owner. Direct any unresolved exception to the Circulation process owner.
