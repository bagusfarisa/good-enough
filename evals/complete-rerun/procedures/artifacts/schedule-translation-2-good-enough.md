# Publish a regional workshop schedule

Keep the last published schedule live until the replacement passes **Review draft**. Publish the replacement once.

## Before you start

Use the translation workbook approved by Localization. Confirm that its **Workbook version** field matches the approval record before generating the draft.

## Build and review the draft

1. Select **Build schedule** to generate a draft.
2. In **Review draft**, compare the dates, venue names, and registration links against the approved workbook. If you find disputed wording or a broken registration link, keep the draft unpublished and follow the applicable steps below.

### Disputed wording

1. Set **Editorial hold**.
2. Send the disputed phrase and workbook version to the Regional editor, who owns the wording decision.
3. Keep **Editorial hold** until the Regional editor confirms the disposition in writing. Retain the confirmation with the workbook.
4. Ask the Schedule procedure owner where to resume after the written confirmation. The procedure leaves this restart point open.

### Broken registration link

1. Notify the Registration team, which owns approved registration URLs.
2. When Registration supplies the approved URL, replace the broken link in the draft.
3. Resume at **Review draft** and repeat all three comparisons.

If both issues apply, follow both sets of steps. Keep **Editorial hold** until the Regional editor confirms the disposition in writing. Ask the Schedule procedure owner how to coordinate the review restart if the link is fixed while the hold is still active.

## Publish and verify

1. Once the draft passes review and any **Editorial hold** is cleared, select **Publish** once.
2. Open the public schedule and verify that it displays the approved dates, venue names, and registration links.
3. After the public check passes, record the published version and publication time.
