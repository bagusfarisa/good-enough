# Loaner laptop returns

## Before the appointment

- Find the loan in the asset register. Check the asset tag and due date.
- Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger. Prepare a reusable equipment bag.

## At intake

1. Scan the asset tag and compare it with the loan record. Check for visible battery swelling before applying power.
2. **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities at extension **221**. Log the asset tag, cabinet location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears it. If the tag also differs, report the discrepancy to the Asset Desk.
3. **Tag mismatch without visible swelling.** Stop processing the return. Set the status to **Check required**, keep the laptop in intake, and refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk. Keep the loan open until the discrepancy is resolved.
4. **Matching tag with no visible swelling.** Check for the charger and inspect the laptop for damage before powering it on. Photograph any surface scratches and continue. Give the borrower a return acknowledgment.

## Process the return

- **Charger missing.** Set the status to **Accessories pending** and record the missing charger in the asset register. Continue normal cleanup and keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery.
- **Complete return.** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. After the tool reports **Reset successful**, set the status to **Returned**, record the receipt timestamp, and place the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

- Review the daily return list. Confirm that each open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner.
- Attach hold updates to the existing record. Use the existing asset register and incident log. Do not create a second loan record for the same return.
