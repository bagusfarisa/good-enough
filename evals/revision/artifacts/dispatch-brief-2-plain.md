# May warehouse dispatch pilot — COO decision brief

Reserve up to **$4,800** for a two-week Atlas pilot starting **May 18** at the **North warehouse, weekday morning shift only**. The working group recommends Atlas because it meets the start date and provides the required timestamped audit log.

The funding decision reserves the budget. Launch requires both approvals below by their deadlines. Shift leaders will continue manual review until launch and keep it available as the fallback throughout the pilot.

## Launch approvals

| Gate | Accountable owner | Deadline | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|---|

## Pilot evaluation and follow-up

After two weeks, compare missed route assignments with the two weeks immediately before the pilot. Continue only if missed assignments fall by **at least 20%** and the audit log has **no gaps**. Any broader rollout requires a separate COO approval.

Record the COO’s funding decision in the normal decision register. At the next checkpoint, review both gate outcomes, cost, audit coverage, and the decisions due after the pilot.
