# Loaner laptop return SOP

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger.
3. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Scan the asset tag** and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, set the status to **Check required** and refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk. Keep the laptop in intake and the loan open until the discrepancy is resolved. Stop the ordinary return process.
2. **Check the charger and inspect the laptop for damage** before powering it on. If you notice visible battery swelling at any point, do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities on extension **221**. Record the tag, location, and time in the incident log. Keep the loan open until Facilities clears it.
3. **Acknowledge receipt** to the borrower when the tag matches and there is no visible battery swelling. If there are only surface scratches, photograph them and continue.
4. **If the charger is missing**, set the status to **Accessories pending** and record the missing charger in the asset register. The laptop can go through normal cleanup. Keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery.
5. **For a complete return**, clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Wait for **Reset successful** before setting the status to **Returned**, recording the receipt timestamp in the asset register, and placing the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

- Review the daily return list. Verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner.
- Attach any hold update to the existing record. Use the asset register and the existing incident log as applicable. Keep one loan record per return.
