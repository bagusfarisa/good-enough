# Loaner laptop returns at intake

## Before the appointment

1. Find the loan in the asset register. Check the recorded asset tag and due date.
2. Ask the borrower to bring the laptop and charger.
3. Prepare a reusable equipment bag for an ordinary return.

## At intake

1. **Verify the asset tag.** Scan the tag and compare it with the loan record. If it differs, set the status to **Check required**, refer the discrepancy to the Asset Desk, and stop. Keep the laptop in intake and leave the loan open until the Asset Desk resolves the discrepancy.
2. **Check the charger and inspect the laptop before powering it on.**
   - **Visible battery swelling.** Do not power on or charge the laptop. Move it to the designated battery isolation cabinet, set the status to **Safety hold**, and call Facilities on extension **221**. Record the asset tag, location, and time in the incident log. Stop and leave the loan open until Facilities clears it.
   - **Surface scratches only.** Photograph the scratches and continue.
3. **Acknowledge receipt.** Give the borrower an acknowledgment once the tag matches and you have checked for battery swelling.
4. **Process the return based on whether the charger is present.**
   - **Charger missing.** Set the status to **Accessories pending** and record the missing charger in the asset register. Keep the loan open while the Asset Desk arranges recovery. The laptop can still go through normal cleanup.
   - **Charger present.** Clear the borrower profile with the approved reset tool. Wait for **Reset successful** before setting the status to **Returned**, recording the receipt timestamp, and placing the laptop on the ready shelf.

## At close of day

Check the daily return list and verify that every open loan in intake has a current status or a recorded owner. Attach any hold updates to the existing record. Use the existing asset register and incident log, and keep one loan record per return.
